New to LaTeX? How to make your own copy of the Overleaf template
  1. Sign in to Overleaf, or create a free account.
  2. Open the template link for your track.
  3. Click Menu (top-left), then Copy Project.
  4. Rename the copy, e.g., MMS200-Surname-ShortTitle, and write only in your copy.

Which documents to submit

MMS 200a (Proposal): Chapters I–IV, describing the planned work, plus References and the Declaration of AI Use. Appendices as needed. Chapters V and VI are not included.

MMS 200b (Completed Special Project): Chapters I–VI, plus References and the Declaration of AI Use. Revise Chapters I–IV to describe what was actually done; substantive changes from the approved proposal need your adviser’s approval.
